Our Sacred Heritage

A timeless place of faith and devotion

Baba Dhabaleswara Mandira is a cherished spiritual destination in Odisha, dedicated to Lord Shiva. The temple represents generations of devotion, sacred traditions and the enduring cultural heritage of the region.

Devotees visit to offer prayers, participate in religious rituals and seek divine blessings. During important festivals, the temple becomes a centre of spiritual gathering and traditional celebration.

Legend & Belief

The story behind Dhabaleswara

The temple's sacred identity is associated with a traditional legend that reflects Lord Shiva's compassion and divine grace.

According to the legend, a sanyassin appeared before an agitated crowd and brought a black calf outside the sanctum. The calf had turned white, helping to dispel the crowd's suspicion. The sanyassin then disappeared, and the man involved understood the divine nature of the encounter.

The transformation of the black calf into white is associated with the name Dhabaleswara, preserving the legend as part of the temple's spiritual tradition.

Art, Culture & History

Where heritage meets spiritual beauty

Odisha's temple heritage is celebrated for its distinctive architecture, traditional craftsmanship and deep spiritual meaning. The Dhabaleswara temple is a place where worship, cultural identity and devotion come together.

Its sacred atmosphere invites devotees to pause, reflect and connect with traditions that continue to shape religious life and community celebrations.
